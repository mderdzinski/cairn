# Cairn v1.1.1

Everything shipped since v1.0.0.

## Improvements

* The reflection banner on Path now looks at the past week - older moments quietly age out of the count - and tapping it takes you straight to the first moment waiting.
* Reflection reminders now arrive only when recent moments are waiting for you.
* On Apple Watch, a small icon appears on the home screen when moments are being kept on-device without iCloud sync.

## Fixes

* Today's stone count, the Today and Yesterday headings on Path, and the Patterns week all move forward correctly when a new day begins.
* Reminders arrive at the right time on daylight-saving days, and keep arriving even if the app hasn't been opened in a while.
* Moments captured on Apple Watch are saved right away, even if you lower your wrist just after.
* The cairn complication stays visible when the watch display dims.
* The sync indicator no longer gets stuck on "Syncing".
* Turning on reminders during onboarding now points you to Settings if notifications were declined earlier.
* Deleting a moment from the reflection sheet closes cleanly.
* Patterns totals now match the daily bars.
