New

- Cairn is made for iPhone Duo. Every screen fits each way you hold it - closed, open, or partly folded.
  - Open, Path shows your moments beside the one you're reflecting on, and Patterns sets your rhythm and breakdown side by side. Larger iPhones held sideways get the same view.
  - Partly folded, Capture keeps the question on one side of the fold and the stones on the other.
  - A reflection you're writing stays put when you fold, unfold, or turn the phone.

Improvements

- Path, Patterns, Reminders, and the welcome screens keep a comfortable reading width on larger displays.
